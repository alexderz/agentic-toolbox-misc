### The Ultimate MoE Training Handbook (Hugging Face)
@Thom_Wolf, Nov 5 2026 ~1:57pm CT. https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2118432475730882627
- **What it is:** Free open handbook on training Mixture-of-Experts models with Megatron-LM, published by Hugging Face as a Space. Subtitle: "How to train a Mixture-of-Experts model at scale, setting by setting, with the runs that back each choice." Covers expert count/size/shared experts, all-to-all communication and hiding latency, silent ~25× slowdowns (e.g. socket fallback vs EFA), router gating (softmax / sigmoid / sqrt(softplus)) and fp32 router precision, auxiliary loss vs DeepSeek-style loss-free expert bias, dense-checkpoint upcycling vs from-scratch, and serving with fewer experts per token than trained with. Chapters: 1 Introduction; 2 Setup and first run (hardware checks, image/EFA build, tokenize FineWeb-Edu, dense ~1.22B baseline, first MoE); 3 Anatomy of MoE training (monitoring, router scoring, routing strategy, load balancing, and further systems material); 4 Serving and related; conclusion and appendix. Companion runnable scripts (first-run sbatch, EFA image build, tokenization, Trackio launcher, router/expert watch hooks) ship as a public GitHub gist. Interactive figure gallery is a separate Space. Prior related HF deep dive referenced in the text: Smol Training Playbook. Announcement framing: 18 of the 20 best open models on the Artificial Analysis index are MoE (two dense exceptions named in the handbook: Nanbeige4.3-33B and Qwen3.8 27B).
