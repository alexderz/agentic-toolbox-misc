# Iris follow-up, 2026-11-18

## 8am run: Tencent Hy4 flash open weights (backlog: fits on paper, needs a draft llama.cpp PR)

- **What it is:** Tencent has now published the Hy4 flash weights that were API-only on Nov 16
  ([earlier follow-up](2026-11-16-followup.md)). 220B total parameters, of which 64B are the Engram n-gram lookup
  table, 12B active per token, text and image input, up to 1M tokens of context.
  [Iris note](https://github.com/alexderz/agentic-toolbox-misc/blob/main/iris/2026-11-18-steady-alex-bookmarks.md) ·
  [tencent/Hy4-flash](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4-flash) ·
  [AngelSlim GGUFs](https://huggingface.co/AngelSlim/Hy4-flash-GGUF) ·
  [llama.cpp PR #32517](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/32517).
- **What we checked (Hugging Face API, read directly):**
  - `tencent/Hy4-flash` @ `d88c9dfd`, ungated: 220.25B parameters in BF16, about 440 GB. `Hy4-flash-FP8` @ `003107fa`
    (about 287 GB) and the pretraining checkpoint `Hy4-flash-base` @ `95b9f823` (219.4B) are also public.
  - Licence: the Tencent Hy License 1.0, which is Apache-2.0 plus commercial terms. A separate licence is needed only
    for a model-as-a-service business over US$50M revenue or 100M monthly users, and internal use is explicitly exempt.
    Unlike some recent custom licences, it has **no territory exclusion**, so it grants us normal use.
  - How it works (from `config.json` and the card): 42 layers (1 dense, 41 MoE). Each token picks 16 of 512 small routed
    experts plus one shared expert, and the experts run in a smaller "latent" width. Attention mixes a 512-token sliding
    window with a DeepSeek-style sparse attention (latent KV, rank 512, with a compressed indexer that picks the top 2048
    positions) at 3 sliding-window layers per sparse layer. That is why the context memory stays small. Engram adds
    hashed bigram and trigram embeddings at layers 2, 12, 22 and 32, stored in FP8. The vision encoder is adapted
    from SigLIP2.
  - GGUFs from Tencent's own compression team (`AngelSlim/Hy4-flash-GGUF` @ `632c8e70`): Q4_K_M 156.8 GiB, Q3_K
    144.7 GiB, IQ2-AH 125.7 GiB, plus two NVIDIA 4-bit float builds aimed at Spark boxes. Every quant ships the
    Engram table as an identical 63.4 GB second shard. The repo's README names an mmproj (vision) file that the repo
    does not contain yet.
  - Engine: stock llama.cpp cannot load it. Support is in PR #32517 (`hy_v4_flash`), which is open, still a draft,
    head `0a054d0b` on Nov 18, and the reviewers have asked the author for another round. SGLang and vLLM recipes are on the card.
- **Local fit on paper (not measured), both GPUs (24 GB + 32 GB) with experts in system RAM:**
  - **Q3_K (about 155 GB on disk): the largest quant that fits.** Roughly 52 GiB of it goes on the cards and about
    93 GiB into RAM, leaving about 20 GiB of RAM for the system and parked sessions.
  - **Q4_K_M (about 168 GB): too tight.** About 105 GiB in RAM leaves too little for parked sessions.
  - **IQ2-AH (about 135 GB): the roomy fallback.**
  - The Engram table only needs a few rows read per token, so it is a natural thing to keep in RAM.
  - With 12B active parameters and the small sparse and sliding-window context memory, this suits our multi-session,
    long-context agent target better than most models of its size, provided the quality holds up.
