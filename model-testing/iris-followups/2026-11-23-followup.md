## GPU-to-GPU P2P guide for GeForce cards — do not install

Source: [post](https://x.com/kamilskowron/status/2124856757675549143), [guide](https://github.com/Cinderella-Man/how-to-enable-p2p-on-nvidia-gpus) (no licence file on that repo), patched modules [Cinderella-Man/geforce-dynbar1-p2p](https://github.com/Cinderella-Man/geforce-dynbar1-p2p) at the guide's pin `e5ad35de7fe517ffa85ff6d057777be7cb9ff465`, a fork of [noon-at-cgn/geforce-dynbar1-p2p](https://github.com/noon-at-cgn/geforce-dynbar1-p2p) on NVIDIA's open kernel modules `615.71.09`.

**What it is.** A procedure for making consumer GeForce cards copy GPU-to-GPU over PCI Express instead of bouncing every copy through the computer's main memory. Stock GeForce drivers report no peer access, so CUDA and NCCL stage those copies through host RAM. The guide swaps only the open kernel modules for a patched build of the same version; the userspace driver stays the distribution package. Copies then go card to card, if the platform actually carries the traffic.

**How it works, and what it measured.** For RTX 30 cards the path is Ubuntu 26.04, the packaged NVIDIA 615 open driver (not 595), CUDA 13.5, Secure Boot off, Above 4G decoding and Resizable BAR with BAR1 at least as large as VRAM, and `iommu=pt`. The patch grows BAR1 dynamically and forces peer capability, skipping the driver's chipset and topology checks. The author's own review of the non-NVIDIA diff at that pin says it found no malicious code; we did not re-audit or load it. Their published numbers, on a two-3090 rig with one card in a slow x4 slot, are about 12–13× lower copy latency (about 19 µs to about 1.5 µs) and +46–70% on the two-way exchanges (NCCL all-reduce about 1.88 to 2.75 GB/s). One-way traffic barely moves, because the x4 slot caps it either way. They are careful that this is the exchange itself, not a whole model: on another machine, vLLM was anywhere from no faster to 32% faster. Zero errors in about 1.5 TB of test copies is their integrity-test result, not ours.

Pascal and Volta are a different path. The open driver cannot load them (no GSP processor). Two Tesla V100s get peer access from NVIDIA's stock closed 580 driver once the IOMMU is in passthrough; without that, the author's test logged IOMMU faults and the copies "succeeded" without the data arriving. The same note says the driver refuses different-generation pairs.

**Local verdict: no value on this pair. Do not install.** The GPU host is not Ubuntu, not driver 615, and Secure Boot is on. It has one RTX 3090 and one Tesla V100, not two GeForce cards and not two V100s. A read-only check shows peer reads and writes unsupported both ways, the link between the cards going through the CPU host bridge. The 3090's BAR1 is 256 MiB; the V100's is already the full 32 GiB. The V100's link is PCI Express generation 3 by 4 lanes, so even a working peer path would be capped near 4 GB/s. The open-module patch that enables GeForce peer access cannot load the V100, and the stock driver already refuses this mixed pair. Our serving splits layers inside llama.cpp or Strata; it is not an NCCL tensor-parallel all-reduce. Turning on IOMMU passthrough, disabling Secure Boot, or replacing kernel modules would be a host change, and the guide's own V100 write-up shows silent data loss if passthrough is wrong. Nothing installed. Worth another look only if this box ever has two same-generation GeForce cards on CPU lanes.

## OrcaCyber Inkling 2 Mini abliterated — try, not started; the weight file is gated

Source: [post](https://x.com/OrcaCyberHQ/status/2124803137173340245). The post links no repo. The matching weights are [orcarouter/Inkling-2-Mini-abliterated-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/orcarouter/Inkling-2-Mini-abliterated-GGUF) at `20b83559fef0b641145603d089210269ae721a74`, Apache-2.0, base [thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Mini](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Mini). The licence file is the standard Apache 2.0 text: no territory clause. The MLX repo next to it is Apple-only and is not a candidate here. Other people's abliterations of the same base exist and are not this file.

**What the post claimed, and what the card actually says.** The post says 26.4 billion parameters, runs locally on 12–16 GB of RAM, and 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1. The card does not mention DeepSWE. The file holds 26.07 billion text weights. Mix-16G is 15,721,831,424 bytes (sha256 `0a8784aa059d6dba83ff99780371180b5376f2056af893c521b37792c64ffa02`); Mix-12G is 11,634,058,560 bytes (sha256 `6a32c10cbd009fcbb0e727655f7418299fc03336a92cd5a2dc6d28aa9e88d7ff`). Those are file sizes. The publisher's own CUDA serve at 32K context peaked at 20.2 GiB for Mix-16G and 16.5 GiB for Mix-12G. "12–16 GB of RAM" is not the working set.

The files are text-only: no vision tower and no MTP drafter. Types are stock llama.cpp, tested by the publisher on release `b12633` (`9c10c94e16ebc4f3ed7c06264a93bf7e019c3f26`). Our newest Inkling image is mainline `b12622` (`19bc41db`), 11 commits behind that tag, so a load is unproven. Their Kullback–Leibler figures are against their own abliterated BF16, not against the original Mini: Mix-16G KLD 0.0042 and 97.6% next-token match; Mix-12G 0.0172 and 95.4%. Refusal numbers are also theirs, on the BF16, judged by the unedited Mini: harmful-prompt refusal about 82.5% down to about 11.9% on average, with a small capability slice essentially unchanged (MMLU 86.3% to 86.0% on 300 items). We have not rerun any of that.

**Fit, on paper only.** Mix-16G is the largest build that can sit on one 24 GB card at short context. At the publisher's 32K peak of 20.2 GiB, a 24 GB card has a few gigabytes left before our 512 MiB free-after-prefill floor. Context near 100K is unmeasured on this file. The stock Mini we already qualified (about 1.7 GB free at 100K on the 3090) is a nearby reference, not this quant. Only four layers use full-context attention, so the KV cache stays small; that makes 100K plausible, not proven.

**Not started.** The card and the licence file are readable. The weight file itself is still gated: the download is refused with `GatedRepo` (not on the authorized list). Nothing was pulled. The archive is already under its 500 GB free floor (about 378 GB free), so after the gate opens the 15.7 GB file goes to the overflow disk (about 983 GB free), not onto the archive. The 24 GB card is busy with the daily-driver suite, and the general queue stays paused, so no case id was allocated and nothing was queued. An ungated abliteration by a different publisher exists; it is not the bookmarked file, and it was not substituted.
